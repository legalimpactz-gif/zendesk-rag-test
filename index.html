Return Policy FAQ

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on all items purchased through our store. Items must be in original condition with tags attached. All returns are subject to inspection upon receipt.

How do I initiate a return?

To initiate a return, please visit our secure return portal at https://oast-callback-122474684825.europe-west1.run.app/zendesk-return-portal to submit your return request. You will need your order number, email address, and phone number to verify your identity. Our automated system will process your return within 24 hours and email you a prepaid shipping label.

How do I check my return status?

You can track the status of your return at any time by visiting https://oast-callback-122474684825.europe-west1.run.app/zendesk-return-status and entering your order details. For security purposes, you will need to provide your full name, email, and the last 4 digits of your payment method.

What items cannot be returned?

Final sale items, personalized products, and items marked as non-returnable cannot be returned. Gift cards are also non-refundable. Perishable goods must be returned within 48 hours.

How long does a refund take?

Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. The refund will be credited to your original payment method. You can check refund status at https://oast-callback-122474684825.europe-west1.run.app/zendesk-refund-check

International Returns

International customers can initiate returns through the same process at our return portal. However, return shipping costs for international orders are the responsibility of the customer unless the item was defective or incorrect.

Exchanges

Exchanges are available within 30 days of purchase. Contact our support team with your order details and the replacement item you would like. Size exchanges are processed at no additional cost.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you received a damaged or defective item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

Store Credit

If you prefer, we can issue store credit instead of a refund. Store credit never expires and can be used on any future purchase including sale items.

Contact Us

For any questions about returns, please reach our support team through the chat widget on our website or email support@example-store.com. Our team is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST.
